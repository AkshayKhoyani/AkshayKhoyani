# Finance Junction – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 28 November 2026_

This policy covers the "Financejunction" Meta app, used only by the owner of the Instagram account **@financejunction.in** to manage that one account.

## What the app does
- Publishes Reels that we create to our own Instagram account (@financejunction.in).
- Reads comments on our own posts and replies to them, or hides spam.
- Reads our own account's insights (views, reach, follower age/city in aggregate) to plan better content.

## What data we access
- Public comment text, comment IDs and usernames of people who comment on our posts.
- Aggregated, anonymous insights that Instagram provides for our own account.

We do **not** collect passwords, phone numbers, email addresses, payment details or private messages. We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

## How long we keep data
Comment and insight data is read at the time of use to write a reply or a report. We do not build profiles of users. Summary numbers (for example, total views of a reel) may be kept in our private notes.

## Your choices and data deletion
You can delete your comment on our post at any time from Instagram, and it will no longer be accessible to the app.
To ask us to remove any reply or information related to you, send a Direct Message to **@financejunction.in** on Instagram with the subject "Data deletion". We will delete it within 7 days and confirm by DM.
