1. Kaise use karein (har naye product ke liye)

  1. Variables nikaalo: ChatGPT/Gemini mein prompts/00-product-variables.txt paste karo (product ka naam daal ke). Jo jawab aaye, use neeche Prompt Builder ke boxes mein bharo.
  2. 6 prompts copy karo: Prompt Builder har photo ka ready prompt bana dega — "Copy" dabao.
  3. Photo banao: ChatGPT (image) ya Gemini mein 2 photos attach karo: assets/tradenest-logo.png + assets/style-reference-pouch.jpg, phir prompt paste karo. Size Square (1:1).
  4. Photo 01 se 05 isi tarah. Photo 06 (Spec Card) ke liye Spec Card Maker use karo — spelling 100% sahi aati hai.
  5. Check karo: naam ki spelling, koi "organic / 100% / No Preservatives" to nahi aaya? Galti ho to prompts/08-fix-mistakes.txt wala sudhaar prompt use karo.
  6. File naam: 01-hero-pouch.jpg, 02-banner.jpg, 03-closeup.jpg, 04-bulk.jpg, 05-uses.jpg, 06-spec-card.jpg — ek product ka ek folder.
  7. PDF: prompts/07-pdf-content.txt se content banao aur Claude ko bhejo — wo same premium PDF bana dega.

Tip: ek product ki saari 5 photos ek hi chat mein banao — style aur pouch design ek jaisa rahega.

7. Final Checklist (har product)

  • ☐ 6 photos, square 1:1, sahi file naam
  • ☐ Logo hubahu sahi + "GOODNESS FROM NATURE"
  • ☐ Product naam ki spelling har photo mein sahi
  • ☐ Koi "organic / 100% / No Preservatives / certificate / health claim" nahi
  • ☐ Chhote jars pe bigda text nahi (ho to plain label waala sudhaar)
  • ☐ Spec card mein specs website se match
  • ☐ MOQ har jagah 5 Kg
  • ☐ PDF bana / Claude ko content bheja
