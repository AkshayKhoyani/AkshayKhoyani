Update Date: 23 November 2026
Organization: Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA)
Exam / Recruitment: Karnataka State Eligibility Test (KSET) 2026
Status: Provisional result declared

The Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA) has declared the KSET Result 2026 on 20 November 2026 along with the subject-wise and category-wise cut off marks. A total of 8,171 candidates have qualified the test.

The General Merit cut off is 158 marks for Kannada and 176 marks for English (out of 300). Candidates can check their qualification status on the KEA website.

How to Check Result

  1. Visit the KEA website cetonline.karnataka.gov.in/kea.
  2. Open the KSET 2026 section.
  3. Click on the result / qualified list link.
  4. Search your roll number in the list or log in with your details.
  5. Download the cut off PDF for your subject and category.
