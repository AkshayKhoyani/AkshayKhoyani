Organization: Staff Selection Commission (SSC) / CAPF
Exam / Recruitment: SSC GD Constable 2026 – Physical Efficiency Test (PET) & Physical Standard Test (PST)
Status: Admit card released

The SSC GD PET PST Admit Card 2026 has been released for candidates who qualified the Constable (GD) Computer Based Test. The admit card was first released on the SSC regional (Central Region) website on 12 November 2026.

The Physical Efficiency Test and Physical Standard Test are being held from 26 November to 11 December 2026. Download the call letter and report to your PET/PST centre on the given date with all documents.

How to Download Admit Card

  1. Visit the official website of your SSC region (or the CAPF recruitment portal).
  2. Click on the SSC GD PET/PST Admit Card 2026 link.
  3. Enter your Registration Number / Roll Number and Date of Birth.
  4. Download the call letter and take two printouts.
  5. Carry it with original documents and photo ID to the PET/PST venue.

Details Mentioned on Admit Card

  • Name, roll number and photo
  • PET/PST date, reporting time and venue
  • List of documents to carry
  • Physical test instructions
