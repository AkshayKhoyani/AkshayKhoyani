Organization: Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs)
Exam / Recruitment: RRB Paramedical Categories (CEN 05/2026) – CBT
Status: Admit card released

The Railway Recruitment Boards have released the RRB Paramedical Admit Card 2026 for CEN 05/2026. The Computer Based Test is scheduled on 23 and 25 November 2026.

The admit card for the 23 November exam was released on 19 November, and the card for the 25 November batch is released four days before that exam. Candidates can download it using their registration number and date of birth.

How to Download Admit Card

  1. Visit the official website of your RRB region.
  2. Click on the CEN 05/2026 (Paramedical) e-Call Letter link.
  3. Log in with your Registration Number and Date of Birth.
  4. Download the e-call letter and take a printout.
  5. Carry it with an original photo ID to the exam centre.

Details Mentioned on Admit Card

  • Candidate name, photo and roll number
  • Exam date, shift and reporting time
  • Exam venue address
  • Exam day instructions
