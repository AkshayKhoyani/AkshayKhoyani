Exam / Recruitment: Sub-Inspector in Delhi Police & CAPFs (CPO) 2026 – Paper 1
Status: Admit card released batch-wise

The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) is releasing the SSC CPO Admit Card 2026 batch-wise for the Sub-Inspector in Delhi Police and Central Armed Police Forces (CPO) Paper 1 Computer Based Test. The exam is being held in shifts and runs till 28 November 2026.

Admit cards are released a few days before each candidate's exam date (for example, the card for the 24 November shift was released on 21 November). Candidates should download it from the official SSC website using their registration details.

How to Download Admit Card

  1. Visit the official website ssc.gov.in and log in.
  2. Open the Admit Card section and select CPO 2026 Paper 1.
  3. Enter your Registration Number / Roll Number and Date of Birth.
  4. Download the admit card and take a clear printout.
  5. Carry the printout with a valid original photo ID to the exam centre.

Details Mentioned on Admit Card

  • Candidate name, roll number and photograph
  • Exam date, shift and reporting time
  • Exam centre name and address
  • Important instructions for the exam day
