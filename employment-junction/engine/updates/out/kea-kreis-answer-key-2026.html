Exam / Recruitment: KREIS (Karnataka Residential Educational Institutions Society) Teacher Recruitment 2026
Status: Provisional answer key released

The Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA) has published the KREIS Provisional Answer Key 2026 on 22 November 2026 for the KREIS teacher recruitment exam.

Objections can be submitted online only till 25 November 2026, 10:30 AM. A fee is charged per question challenged, and the final key will be released after expert review.
