## F. Aam aadmi par asar (illustrative calculation, prediction nahi)
Agar repo +25 bps aur bank poora badhaav loan par de (repo-linked / EBLR loans par jaldi lagta hai):
| Loan | Abhi @ 8.00% (example) | +25 bps (8.25%) | +50 bps (8.50%) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Home loan ₹50 lakh, 20 saal** | ₹41,822/mahina | ₹42,603 (**+₹781**) | ₹43,391 (**+₹1,569**) |
| Car/personal ₹10 lakh, 5 saal | ₹20,276 | ₹20,396 (+₹120) | ₹20,517 (+₹240) |
- ₹50 lakh home loan par +25 bps poore 20 saal mein **~₹1.87 lakh extra** (agar rate wahi rahe).
- **FD wale:** Rates badhne par banks aksar FD rates bhi badhate hain (bank par depend).
- **Stocks jin par asar dikh sakta hai:** Banks, NBFC / housing finance, real estate, auto (rate-sensitive). Ye general pattern hai, prediction nahi.
