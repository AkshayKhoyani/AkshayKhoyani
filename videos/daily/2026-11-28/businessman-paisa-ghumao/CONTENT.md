# Reel: "Businessman paise se paisa kaise banata hai?" (CONTENT, reel abhi nahi)
Length: ~40 sec (content ~38.5s + branding 1–1.5s). Format: skit, characters: **Chintu** (sawaal), **Gurpreet Paaji** (businessman, samjhata hai), **EMI Bhoot** (cameo).
Numbers sirf **example/hisaab** hain ("maan lo"), kisi asli return ka daava nahi. Screen par: "Example ke liye numbers · Not advice".

## Hook (0–3s)
Screen: **"₹1 LAKH → ₹40,000 HAR MAHINE? 🤯"** (bada, pehle frame se)
Chintu `[shocked]`: "Paaji! Ek lakh se har mahine chaalis hazaar?!"

## Raaz #1: Paisa ghumao (3–14s)
Paaji `[laughs]`: "Haan Chintu! Business ka pehla raaz: paisa ghumao."
Paaji: "Ek lakh ka maal liya, das percent munafe pe becha: das hazaar. Mahine mein chaar baar ghumaya? Chaalis hazaar!"
Screen: ₹ sikke ek gol chakkar mein ghoomte hain, har chakkar par "+₹10,000" → counter 10k, 20k, 30k, **40k**.
Hisaab: 1,00,000 × 10% = 10,000 × 4 = **40,000** ✅

## Raaz #2: Udhaar ka time (14–23s)
Paaji: "Doosra raaz: supplier ko paisa tees din baad dena hai, aur maal das din mein bik gaya. Matlab beech ke din, dhandha supplier ke paise se!"
Screen: calendar: **"Supplier ko: 30 din"** vs **"Maal bika: 10 din"** → beech ke **20 din** gold mein highlight.
(Asli concept: credit period / working capital. Badi retail companies bhi isi par chalti hain.)

## Raaz #3: Munafa wapas lagao (23–30s)
Paaji: "Teesra: munafa udao mat, dhande mein wapas lagao. Maal bada, toh munafa bada."
Screen: ₹1 lakh ka dabba → ₹1.1 lakh → ₹1.2 lakh (badhta stack).

## Twist (30–35s)
EMI Bhoot hawa mein aata hai `[whispers]`: "Par maal na bika… toh yahi udhaar, main ban jaaunga!" 👻
Chintu `[scared]`: "Aaah!"
Paaji `[calm]`: "Isiliye hisaab pakka, aur udhaar utna hi jitna chuka sako."

## CTA (35–38.5s)
Chintu: "Kaunsa raaz best? Ek, do ya teen, comment karo!"
Screen: "1️⃣ 2️⃣ 3️⃣ 👇"
