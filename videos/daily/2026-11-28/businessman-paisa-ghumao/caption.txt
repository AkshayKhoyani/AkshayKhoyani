🤯 ₹1 lakh se har mahine ₹40,000? Businessman ka "paisa ghumao" formula!

1️⃣ Paisa ghumao: ₹1 lakh ka maal, 10% munafa, mahine mein 4 baar = ₹40,000
2️⃣ Udhaar ka time: supplier ko 30 din baad, maal 10 din mein bika
3️⃣ Munafa wapas dhande mein lagao

⚠️ Par maal na bika to wahi udhaar "EMI Bhoot" ban jaata hai. Hisaab pakka rakho!
