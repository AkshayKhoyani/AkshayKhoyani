## Posting tips

- **Frequency:** 3–4 reels/week. Suggested order: 9 (intro) → 1 → 3 → 6 → 4 → 2 → 8 → 10 → 7 → 5 (post 5 in Jan–Feb).
- **Best times (India):** 7–9 PM weekdays, 11 AM–1 PM weekends. Check your own Insights after 2 weeks.
- **Hook rule:** text must appear in the first second. Keep it big and in the middle.
- **Captions:** always add on-screen subtitles. Many people watch without sound.
- **Keyword CTAs** ("COVER", "SIP", "TAX") make DMs easy to track. Reply within 1 hour.
- **Cover image:** use the hook text on a branded colour so the profile grid looks consistent.
- **Compliance:** never say "guaranteed returns" for market-linked products, and keep the disclaimer in each caption. If you are an AMFI-registered distributor, add your ARN in the bio.
